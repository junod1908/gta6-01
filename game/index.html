SOLMAR BAY

An open-world crime game — a city, a car, and a debt to pay.

You are Rico Vega, a driver for hire in Solmar Bay. Mara Solis has four jobs for you — fares, a stolen coupe, a heat wave, a harbor run. Steal cars, outrun the bay PD, and finish the story. The city, the cars and the people are all yours to use.

WASD drive & walk  ·  Mouse drag: camera  ·  Click: shoot  ·  E: enter / exit car  ·  F: punch  ·  Space: jump / handbrake  ·  Shift: run  ·  P: pause  ·  M: mute

PAUSED

Solmar Bay waits for no one — except right now.

BUSTED

The bay PD took you in. You lose 10% of your cash and wake up at the safehouse. Mara is not happy.
